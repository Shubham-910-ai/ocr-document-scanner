Technical Architecture

How The Computer Vision Pipeline Works

A deep dive into OpenCV image processing algorithms and Tesseract OCR integration.

1. Pre-Processing & Canny Edges
cv2.Canny & cv2.GaussianBlur

The input photo is converted to single-channel Grayscale and smoothed using a 5x5 Gaussian Kernel to reduce digital camera noise. Canny Edge Detection isolates steep intensity gradients to expose the sharp borders of paper against backgrounds.

2. Contour & Polygon Fitting
cv2.findContours & cv2.approxPolyDP

OpenCV retrieves continuous closed boundary contours. Contours are sorted by surface area, and Ramer-Douglas-Peucker algorithm (approxPolyDP) simplifies curves into a 4-point polygon representing the document sheet.

3. 4-Point Perspective Warp
cv2.getPerspectiveTransform

Corner points are ordered (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left). A 3x3 transformation matrix calculates perspective mapping, warping trapezoidal angled document photos into flat, top-down rectangular views.

4. Binarization & Enhancement
cv2.adaptiveThreshold & CLAHE

Adaptive Gaussian thresholding computes local thresholds across small pixel neighborhoods. Shadows, glares, and background blemishes are completely eliminated, leaving high-contrast dark text on a pure white page.

5. Tesseract Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
pytesseract & LSTM Neural Networks

The enhanced document image is passed to the Tesseract OCR engine, utilizing Line-level LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) recurrent neural networks to recognize characters, layout structure, word bounding boxes, and confidence percentages.

Layout Analysis (PSM) Page Segmentation Mode identifies text blocks and paragraphs.
Confidence Analysis Computes character probability metrics per recognized word.
Multi-Language Support Supports English, Spanish, French, German, and 100+ languages.
